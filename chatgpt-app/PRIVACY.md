# Privacy Policy — Visual Eye for ChatGPT

**Effective date:** 16 November 2026  
**Developer:** Mohammad Hossein Azad  
**Contact:** mohammadhosseinazad3@gmail.com

This policy applies to the Visual Eye ChatGPT MCP service at `https://visual-eye-v.onrender.com/mcp` and its touch interface.

## Information processed

The MCP service receives only the structured workflow fields defined by its tool, such as the selected workflow stage, interface title and text, option labels, option commands, and optional analysis text. The service does not accept or upload image-file bytes through its MCP tool schema.

When you attach an image to a ChatGPT conversation, image handling and model analysis take place through ChatGPT and are subject to OpenAI's applicable terms and privacy controls. The Visual Eye MCP server is not designed to receive or store the attached image itself.

## Purposes and use

The service uses the submitted workflow fields only to return the requested Visual Eye interface state to ChatGPT. It does not use those fields for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated purposes.

## Storage and retention

The Visual Eye application does not maintain a user database, account profile, image archive, or persistent storage of MCP tool payloads. Tool requests are handled as stateless requests and the application returns the requested interface data.

The hosting provider may process limited technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, such as connection metadata and error logs. Such infrastructure records are governed by the hosting provider's policies and retention practices; the Visual Eye developer does not control the provider's operational log retention.

## Recipients and service providers

- **OpenAI / ChatGPT:** processes the conversation and any images you choose to attach under OpenAI's applicable terms and privacy policy.
- **Render:** hosts the MCP endpoint and may process technical service metadata as described in Render's policies.

The Visual Eye MCP service does not intentionally send tool payloads to analytics providers, advertising networks, or external image-processing APIs.

## User choices and controls

You choose whether to invoke Visual Eye and what text or images to share in your ChatGPT conversation. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive information unless you have reviewed the policies and controls of the services involved. You can stop using the app at any time.

## Security

The service uses HTTPS and a stateless MCP endpoint. No service can guarantee absolute security; report suspected vulnerabilities or privacy issues through the support contact below.
